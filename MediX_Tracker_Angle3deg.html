Biceps curl

Strap the IMU on your biceps (upper arm). EMG 1 goes on the biceps, EMG 2 on the front shoulder. Keep the upper arm still and straight.

1. Calibrate

9 seconds, 3 steps, a beep marks each: (1) arm straight down, relax. (2) raise the straight arm forward to shoulder height. (3) flex your biceps as hard as you can.
